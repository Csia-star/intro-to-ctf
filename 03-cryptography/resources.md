# Cryptography: Resources

- [CryptoHack](https://cryptohack.org) (free, the best place to learn modern crypto by doing)
- [Introduction to CryptoHack course](https://cryptohack.org/courses/intro/) (encoding + XOR)
- [CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/) (decode anything in the browser)
- [picoCTF picoGym, Cryptography](https://play.picoctf.org/practice) (Mod 26, caesar, and more)
- [CTF101: Cryptography](https://ctf101.org/cryptography/overview/)
- [dCode cipher identifier](https://www.dcode.fr/cipher-identifier)

> CryptoHack shows community solutions **after** you solve a challenge. Use them to compare methods, not to skip the challenge.
