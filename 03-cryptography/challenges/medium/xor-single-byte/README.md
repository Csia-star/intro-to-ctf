## Concepts required

Bytes and hex (from Easy), XOR and its properties, brute force, known plaintext. See [concepts 1, 2, 6–8](../../../concepts/README.md).

## Your mission

A message was XORed with **one secret byte**. Find that byte and recover the flag.
