# Hints (open one at a time)

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Start with reverse image search. Try more than one engine — Google Lens, Yandex, and TinEye each win on different images. Yandex is often strongest for places and buildings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Read everything *in* the photo: shop names, languages, scripts, phone-number formats, car plates, flags. Each one narrows the country or city.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 3</summary>

Once you suspect a city or landmark, open Google Maps and switch to **Street View** to match the exact angle, building, and surroundings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 4</summary>

For "what is this" questions, reverse image search usually names the landmark directly. For "where exactly", pivot: landmark → neighbourhood → precise corner in Street View. Cross-check two features before answering.
</details>
