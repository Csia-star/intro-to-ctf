# Hints (open one at a time)

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Before any fancy tool, get the image and read its **metadata**. An EXIF viewer (or `exiftool`) may reveal an author/owner name or GPS.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

That name looks like a **username**. The same handle is often reused across platforms. Search it everywhere (try whatsmyname.app, and a plain search engine).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 3</summary>

Open the public profiles you find (a blog, a social account, a code profile). Read them carefully — avatars, bios, and posts answer several of the room's questions, including a location.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 4</summary>

One answer (a "password") is hidden in plain sight on one of those pages — look at the page itself, not just the obvious text (consider a blog post's content and even the page's styling/colours as a hint the room gives). Pivot from each finding to the next.
</details>
