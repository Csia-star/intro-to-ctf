# Hints (open one at a time)

<details>
<summary>Hint 1 (Tier: who)</summary>

Start with the given image's **metadata**. If it was stripped, fall back to **reverse image search** to find where the picture lives online and the handle that posted it.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2 (Tier: handle)</summary>

Take the handle and enumerate it across platforms (whatsmyname.app, search engines). The person's public profiles hold their name and more.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 3 (Tier: where)</summary>

Photos on those profiles can be **geolocated**: read signs/landmarks and confirm in Street View. Cross-check the city against what the profiles claim.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 4 (Tier: deeper)</summary>

Later tiers pivot to other public records — for example a **public cryptocurrency address** on a blockchain explorer, or **archived** versions of a page (Wayback Machine). Follow each clue to the next; every tier builds on the previous answer.
</details>
