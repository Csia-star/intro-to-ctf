- [CTF101: not applicable — see TryHackMe's OSINT path instead](https://tryhackme.com)

> **Ethics reminder:** these tools are for challenge targets and authorised engagements only. Using them against a real private person can cause real harm and may be illegal. Re-read [rules and ethics](../00-getting-started/ctf-rules-and-ethics.md).
