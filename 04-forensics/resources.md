# Digital Forensics: Resources

- [picoCTF picoGym, Forensics category](https://play.picoctf.org/practice?category=4) (free account, our three challenges)
- [CTF101: Forensics](https://ctf101.org/forensics/overview/) (free reading, good explanations of file formats)
- [Wireshark official user guide](https://www.wireshark.org/docs/wsug_html_chunked/) (free)
- [ExifTool documentation](https://exiftool.org) (official)
- [CyberDefenders](https://cyberdefenders.org) (blue-team and forensics labs; free tier plus paid content)
- [hexed.it](https://hexed.it) (hex editor in the browser)

> picoCTF publishes hints inside each challenge page, and the community posts writeups after each event. Read them **after** you try.
