# Reverse Engineering: Resources

- [picoCTF picoGym, Reverse Engineering](https://play.picoctf.org/practice?category=3) (free account, our three challenges)
- [Ghidra (official, free, by the NSA)](https://ghidra-sre.org) (decompiler)
- [GDB documentation](https://www.gnu.org/software/gdb/documentation/) (official)
- [pwndbg](https://github.com/pwndbg/pwndbg) (GDB plugin that makes output far friendlier)
- [Decompiler Explorer](https://dogbolt.org) (runs several decompilers on your binary in the browser)
- [crackmes.one](https://crackmes.one) (endless practice crackmes by difficulty)
- [CTF101: Reverse Engineering](https://ctf101.org/reverse-engineering/overview/) (free reading)

> Read community writeups **after** you try. Reversing is a skill that only grows by struggling with the decompiler yourself first.
